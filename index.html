Kirby Inventory System

Powering your Kirby distributorship with modern tracking, accountability and insights.

Inventory Tracking

Keep a precise record of every Kirby unit in your office:

  • Unique serial number required for each unit
  • Status and location tracking (in store, issued, in demo, sold retail, sold distributor, returned, in repair, missing)
  • Movement logs capture every transfer, issue and status change
  • Search and filter to find any unit instantly

Demo Logging & Verification

Empower dealers to track demos and accountability:

  • Log daily demos and sales together (e.g. 2/1 or K-2/1)
  • Photo proof required when demos exceed sales (pad photos with duplicate detection)
  • Client information and objection notes capture why deals didn't close
  • Soft-mode verification: dealers are alerted to missing proof but can submit; managers review and approve later

Sales & Commission Engine

Automate your commission calculations and split rules:

  • Configure commission percentages per payment type (cash, credit, debit, check, A-Loan, B-Loan, other)
  • Multi-role commissions: dealer, closer, translator, team leader, manager, canvasser
  • Customizable bonuses for volume, knock sales, weekly demos and team performance
  • Real-time commission totals for each sale and daily summaries

Accessory Tracking & Transfers

Extend accountability beyond units:

  • Manage hoses, shampoo systems, belts, filters and bags with optional serial tracking
  • Set low-stock alerts so you never run out of essentials
  • Log distributor transfers and trainee sales with separate reporting

Reporting & Dashboard

See your office health at a glance:

  • Real-time dashboard with inventory counts, sales metrics, demo activity and reconciliation alerts
  • Dealer performance panels with closing rate, knock conversion, objection patterns and commission earned
  • Daily automatic email reports summarizing sales, revenue, demo counts, unverified demos and suspicious activity
  • Export data to Excel for deeper analysis

Security & Roles

Control access and maintain integrity:

  • Role-based permissions: owner, manager, team leader, dealer
  • Row-level security ensures dealers see only their own data
  • Audit logs record every action: inventory movements, commission rule changes, status updates
